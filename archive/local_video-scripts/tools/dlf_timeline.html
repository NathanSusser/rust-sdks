What this can and can't tell you

It shows which log types switch on, off, or scale with the video. It does not show what the records say. Grant sizes, MCS, rank and buffer status sit inside Qualcomm's proprietary record layouts, which only QCAT or QXDM decode. Log types are left unnamed on purpose: an earlier guess at ID ranges produced a wrong conclusion.

Counts are lower bounds. QCSuper drops frames with bad checksums, at a rate that varies between runs and drifts within one.
